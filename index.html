About

A young composer
at the desk.

Andrew Trotter is an eighteen-year-old composer and pianist. His catalog spans symphony, ballet, string quartet, sacred collections, and solo piano, with eight opus numbers to date and new work arriving each season.

His work has been recognized in regional competitions, including a first-place finish at the Georgia Music Festival and a second place at the Bob Jones University piano competition. He continues to study, perform, and release new work independently.

His music sits in the slower-listening tradition, written to be sat with rather than skimmed, drawing on the line that runs from the European masters through the cinematic composers of today.

Currently Freshman at Kennesaw State University. At the desk on Vision Fugitives (Op. VIII, 24 pieces), with String Quartet No. 3 in engraving.
