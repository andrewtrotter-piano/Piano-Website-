i. The Composer

A piano at the window. A page half-written. The distance between what the score shows and what the hand is reaching for — that's where the work lives.

I write for the room before I write for the archive. Pieces that want to be heard, not catalogued.

II. About the
Composer

Andrew Trotter is a composer and pianist writing from Suwanee, Georgia. His work spans symphony, ballet, chamber, and solo piano — a catalog arriving one piece at a time, at the grand in his room, mostly after dark.

He writes in the long shadow of the composers he loves — Bach, Chopin, Williams, Shaw — and he writes to be played. Each work has an audience in mind before it has a form.

Formally trained from childhood, now in his first year of college study. The catalog continues.
